# PLV Dance Company — Attendance Policies

Effective on adoption by the Executive Board.

## 1. Class Schedule
Every **Friday and Saturday**, **5:30 PM – 8:30 PM**.

## 2. Attendance Recording

| Status | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Present** | Checked in before **5:45 PM** |
| **Late** | Checked in after **5:45 PM** |
| **Absent** | No check-in by **8:30 PM** |
| **Excused** | Approved by Executive Board in advance |

Check-in opens at **5:00 PM** (30 minutes before class) and closes at **8:30 PM**.

## 3. Late Policy
- **Three (3) late check-ins = one (1) absence** (rolling across the semester).
- Late arrivals must still check in.

## 4. Excused Absences
- Accepted reasons: medical, family emergency, school-sanctioned event.
- Must notify the Executive Board **before** class — or within 24 hours for emergencies.
- Documentation required for medical and school events.
- Excused absences do not count against the attendance rate.

## 5. Provisionary Members
- Must maintain **≥ 75% attendance** to be eligible for promotion to Official Member.
- Evaluated monthly by the Executive Board.
- Below 75% = extended probation or removal.

## 6. Anti-Cheating Rules

### 6.1 One Account Per Person
- Each member has one account tied to their unique student ID.
- **Sharing accounts is strictly prohibited.**

### 6.2 Rotating QR Codes
- QR codes **change every 30 seconds**.
- Screenshots, photos, and shared codes **will not work** — codes expire within one window.
- Manual codes (for camera failures) expire in the same 30-second window.

### 6.3 Device Binding
- Your account binds to the device you first check in from.
- Using a different device will be **flagged for Executive Board review**.
- To legitimately reset your device, contact an Executive Board member.

### 6.4 Collusion Detection
- Multiple check-ins from the same IP address or network within a short window are **flagged**.
- This is a warning flag, not immediate discipline — it triggers review.

### 6.5 Prohibited Actions
- Logging into another member's account
- Scanning another member's QR
- Sharing QR codes or codes via text, screenshot, or screen recording
- Automation or scripts to check in
- Any attempt to falsify attendance

### 6.6 Consequences

| Offense | Consequence |
|---|---|
| 1st | Warning. Attendance for that class is **voided**. |
| 2nd | Full semester attendance audited. Written reprimand. |
| 3rd | Removal from the company, disciplinary referral to the school. |

## 7. Privacy

**Data collected:** name, student ID, section, attendance records, device fingerprint, IP address.
**Purpose:** attendance tracking, anti-cheating detection, contact tracing.
**Access:** Executive Board only.
**Retention:** current semester + 1 year, then purged.

## 8. Appeals

- Attendance disputes must be filed within **7 days** of the class.
- Process: contact any Executive Board member with supporting evidence.
- Decision: majority vote of the Executive Board.

## 9. Contact Tracing

Used only for health incidents or injury follow-ups.
Shared only with authorized parties (e.g., school health office).

## 10. Amendments

The Executive Board may amend these policies with **7 days' notice** through the attendance portal.
